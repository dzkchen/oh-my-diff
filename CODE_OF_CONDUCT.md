# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in Oh My Diff's issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to be respectful and
constructive, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption.

The maintainer, [@dzkchen](https://github.com/dzkchen), enforces this and may edit or remove contributions,
lock threads, or block participants, starting with a warning except in severe cases. Report a problem privately
through [the private reporting form](https://github.com/dzkchen/oh-my-diff/security/advisories/new) with "Conduct"
in the title; reports are kept confidential.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version
2.1.
